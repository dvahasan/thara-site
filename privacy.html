Privacy policy

Last updated 12 September 2026

The short version. Thara keeps your financial records in an encrypted database on your phone. The developer runs no server and cannot see your data. Backups are encrypted on the phone and written only where you point them, and only you hold the passphrase. The one thing that leaves your control is anything you choose to type into the feedback form, which is a Google Form and is described below.

What Thara stores

Thara stores the sources, records, types, obligations, plans, gold holdings, exchange rates and settings you enter. All of it lives in a SQLCipher encrypted database on your device. The encryption key is generated on the device and kept in the Android Keystore or iOS Keychain. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the developer, because there is none.

Network access

Thara declares internet access for exactly two features, and both can be switched off:

  • Rate updates. Off until you switch them on. Fetches the published dollar rate and world gold price from a public, key-less currency API. The request contains the currency being asked about and nothing else: no balance, no identifier, no account.
  • The update check. On by default, because Thara is installed as a file rather than from a store and nothing else would ever tell you that a fix has shipped. Once a day the app fetches version.json from this website and compares the build number in it with its own. The request is a plain GET for a public file: it carries no identifier, no account and nothing about your wallet, and the reply is the same bytes for everyone. You can switch it off under Settings, About.

With both features off, the app makes no network requests at all. Thara contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

Links the app can open

Settings carries a few links: the feedback form, the download page, and this policy. These are not a third network feature. Tapping one hands the address to whatever your phone uses for links and that is the end of Thara's part in it — the app fetches nothing, sends nothing, and attaches nothing about your wallet to the address.

What happens after that is between you and the site you opened. The feedback form is a Google Form, so anything you type into it is held by Google and by the developer in a Google spreadsheet, under Google's privacy policy rather than this one. It asks for no account and no name, and the only required question is what went wrong. Please leave real amounts, balances and account numbers out of it: knowing what happened is enough, and nothing you write there has the protection that the data on your phone has.

Google Drive backup

Not currently offered. Drive backup is switched off in the app: there is no card to connect an account, no sign-in is attempted, and no upload runs. The section below describes how it behaves if it is offered again, and applies to nothing while it is off. Automatic backups into a folder you choose cover the same ground and involve no account anywhere.

If you connect a Google account, Thara asks for a single permission: access to its own application data folder on your Google Drive. This is the drive.appdata scope. That folder is hidden from the regular Drive interface and is readable only by Thara. Thara cannot see, read, list or change any other file on your Drive, and it does not read your Google profile beyond the email address shown in Settings so you can tell which account is connected.

Before a backup is uploaded it is encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from a passphrase you choose. The passphrase is never stored in the backup, never sent to Google, and never sent to the developer. Without it the backup cannot be read. If you forget it, no one can recover the backup for you.

You can disconnect the Google account from the app's Settings at any time. You can also revoke Thara's access from your Google account's third-party access page, and delete the backup from Google Drive under Settings, Manage apps.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Thara's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to store and retrieve your own encrypted backups, is never transferred to anyone else, and is never used for advertising.

Other backups and exports

You can also save encrypted backups to a folder you choose or share them to another app. Files handed to the system save dialog go wherever you point them; Thara does not learn the destination and keeps no copy. Plain JSON and CSV exports are deliberately unencrypted, so treat them as you would any file containing your finances.

Device permissions

  • Biometrics. Only if you enable fingerprint or face unlock. The app receives a yes or no from the operating system and never sees biometric data.
  • Notifications. Only if you enable reminders, and requested at that moment rather than at first launch.
  • Storage. Folder backups use the system document picker with a permission on one folder. No general storage permission is requested.

Children

Thara is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Since it collects nothing, there is nothing to collect.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Because Thara has no server, the app cannot notify you of policy changes itself.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer at the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.

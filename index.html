What it does

Everything a finance app should do, and the paperwork read for you

Twenty-four areas, grouped by what you would open the app to do. One line each; the app is where the detail lives.

Money in and out

Sources المصادرCash, banks, wallets, cards and loans, each in its own currency.
Records السجلاتIncome, spending and transfers, with notes, search and filters.
Say it قلها بصوتكHold the microphone like a voice note and say the record, in Arabic or English, and Thara fills in the form for you to check. Written down on the phone; in Enhanced AI mode the sound goes online to Thara AI to be written down.
Talk to Thara تحدّث مع ثراءThe + button opens every quick action and the assistant. Type or say a record and Thara drafts it, asks for anything missing, and saves it only when you say "save it". Questions about your wallet are answered from your records, with charts; anything else is answered online by Thara AI, with its sources shown. Or talk to it live, as on a call.
Read a receipt قراءة الفاتورةPhotograph a receipt, invoice or card slip: Thara reads the shop, the total, the date, the time and the kind of paper, and points out a blurred photo or faded print. Read on the phone; in Enhanced AI mode the photo also goes online for Thara AI to read.
Bank messages رسائل البنكTurn it on and Thara reads the messages your banks, wallets, InstaPay and card companies send, on the phone, and turns each into a record for the right source, by itself or for your approval. Codes, offers, personal messages and anything from a phone number are left alone.
Types الأنواعYour own categories, each with an optional monthly cap.
Obligations الالتزاماتRent, instalments and bills with due dates. Paying one writes the record.

Where you stand

Analysis التحليلCharts for any stretch of time: savings rate, where it went by type and by source, needs against wants.
Looking ahead التخطيطPick two dates; months that cannot pay for themselves are named.
Real wealth قيمة ثروتكNet worth in your currency, in grams of gold and in dollars.
Goals الأهدافA car, a trip, a wedding or Hajj: an amount by a date, followed from the balances you pick, paused when life needs it.
Emailed reports تقارير بالبريدYour balances, dues and goals in your inbox, weekly or monthly, if you ask for them.

Gold, dollars and zakat

Gold you hold ذهبكBy weight and karat, valued at today's price, not a number you rewrite.
Zakat الزكاةThe holding year tracked for you, with the nisab and the working shown.

The detail

The detail, when you want it

Open one and the last one closes, so this page never gets long again.

SecurityAES-256 on the phone, sealed in your account, and online only for what you are told about first. 6 controls
  • Encrypted at restSQLCipher, AES-256. The key is 256 bits from the device's own generator, held in the Android Keystore. Copying the file off the phone yields ciphertext.
  • In your Thara accountA sealed copy of your wallet is kept in Thara's cloud, so a new phone signs in and has everything back. Thara's service keeps the key to it, locked with a master key of its own: that is how a reset by email or a Google sign-in can always bring your wallet back. It hands the key only to a phone that has proved it is you. Holding the key, it could open your wallet; it is built not to.
  • App lockA 4-digit PIN, hashed with a random salt, with fingerprint or face unlock on top. Wrong attempts cool down. Forget it and your account proves it is you: you set a new PIN and nothing is erased. The PIN is not the encryption key.
  • Nothing leaksScreenshots, recording and the recent-apps thumbnail are blocked. Hide-amounts turns every figure to dots. Auto-lock from immediately to never.
  • What goes onlineYour account and its sealed copy. Thara AI, only in the ways it asks you about first. Family accounts you join. Today's rates, if you turn them on. One public file a day, to see whether there is a newer build. Reports of errors and freezes, with nothing of your records in them. The reports you ask to have emailed. Nothing else, and no ads or analytics.
  • Asked, in plain wordsThe microphone, camera, text messages, photos and notifications are each asked for only when you use the feature, with what it is for. The text of your bank messages is read on the phone and never sent anywhere.
Seventeen plansGraded against your real records, not a questionnaire you filled in once. 17 plans

Run as many at once as you like. Each gives a status, a progress bar, the numbers behind it and specific advice. Projections are labelled as projections, at the return you assume.

0150/30/20 ruleGrades the month's needs, wants and saving split against the targets.
02Monthly limitsPer-type caps, with a warning before you go over.
03Expense planA ceiling for the month, with the pace that says where it is heading.
04Income planWhat you mean to earn each month, from all incomes or the ones you pick.
05Six jarsEvery income split six ways, from necessities to giving.
06Emergency fundA target from your actual average spending, not a guess.
07Savings goalAn amount by a date, and the monthly figure to reach it.
08Pay yourself firstSet aside a share of income before spending any of it.
09Invest every monthRegular investing projected at your assumed return.
10Financial independenceYour FI number, and how long to reach it in real terms.
11Get out of debtOrder your debts and see the exact debt-free month.
12Zakat2.5% of zakatable wealth against the nisab, working shown.
1352-week challengeA small amount in week one, a little more every week, for a year.
14100 envelopesEnvelopes 1 to 100, each filled with its number times an amount.
15No-spend challengeOnly what you need, for a set number of days; every day kept turns green.
16Save every dayA small fixed amount put aside every single day.
17Step-up savingKeep a small share of income, and raise it a little every month.
Backups locked with your accountEncrypted on the phone first, then written to a folder you chose or your Google Drive, on a schedule. 3 steps
  1. Encrypted on the phoneAES-256-GCM, with a key that comes from your Thara account: no passphrase to choose and no code to write down.
  2. Written where you choseInto a folder on the phone, or Thara's own hidden folder in your Google Drive. Older automatic copies are pruned.
  3. Restore by signing inOn any phone, sign in to your Thara account and the backup opens. Restore runs in one transaction, so an interrupted one leaves the old wallet intact.

Thara writes the file and nothing else. Whether the folder sits on the phone, an SD card or inside a cloud app you already have is your choice and that app's business. In Google Drive, Thara can open only its own hidden folder, and the file there is encrypted, so Google cannot read it. Backups made by earlier builds, with a passphrase or a recovery code, still open with that passphrase or code.

Installing itOne tap on the phone. Later builds install over the top and keep your wallet. 4 steps

Android Ready to use

  1. Open this page on the phonePress Download APK.
  2. Allow the installAndroid asks because the file did not come from the Play Store.
  3. Open it and sign inMake your Thara account with an email or Google, or sign in to the one you have: your wallet comes back with it.
  4. Choose a PINFour digits, with fingerprint or face on top if you like.
Coming from an older Thara? Install over the top

The new build installs over the old one and keeps everything. Never delete the old app first: until your wallet is in an account, it lives only on that phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

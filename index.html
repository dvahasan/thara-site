Everything a finance app should do, without the cloud

Thara is a complete personal finance manager. It was built for people who want the full picture of their money and do not want to hand that picture to anyone.

Sources المصادر

Cash, bank accounts, mobile wallets, cards, savings, investments, money owed to you and debts. Each in its own currency, with balances computed from your records rather than stored and drifted.

Records السجلات

Expenses, income and transfers between your own sources. Transfers are tracked but kept out of spending analysis, because moving money between your pockets is not spending it.

Types الأنواع

Sort spending however you like. Each type has an icon, a colour, an optional monthly limit and a needs, wants or saving class. About 35 Arabic and English types are ready on first run.

Obligations الالتزامات

Rent, instalments, utilities, loans, subscriptions and tax, each with its due date and repeat rule. Paying one writes the record for you. Standing orders log themselves once their date has passed.

Analysis التحليل

Monthly and yearly views: income, spending, savings rate, where it went by type, needs, wants and saving split, biggest changes, and net worth over the last twelve months.

Looking ahead التخطيط

Pick two dates and Thara walks the months between them: expected income, ordinary costs, what falls due and where net worth ends up. Months that cannot pay for themselves are called out by name.

Dollar and gold الدولار والذهب

Pick your market, record what a dollar and a gram actually cost, or fetch the published rates once a day. A rate you typed is never overwritten by a downloaded one.

Gold you hold ذهبك

Physical metal by weight and karat, not as a balance you keep rewriting. Thara shows what it is worth if sold and what buying it again would cost. The gap is the workmanship you paid.

Real wealth قيمة ثروتك

Net worth month by month in your currency, in grams of pure gold and in dollars, each month at the rate that held then. A balance up thirty per cent has not grown if it buys less gold.

Zakat الزكاة

Arithmetic, not a ruling. 2.5% of qualifying wealth against a gold or silver nisab over a 354-day lunar year. The plan tracks your holding year; the calculator works from figures you type.

Reminders التنبيهات

Off until you switch them on. A bill falling due, one already overdue, a zakat year completing, a spending cap passed, or a backup that has not run in a long time. Each one separately.

Arabic and English عربي وإنجليزي

Full right-to-left layout, optional Arabic-Indic numerals, and a first-run guide you can reopen from Settings at any topic. Try it with two years of sample data before entering anything.

Nine plans, graded against your real records

Run as many as you like at once. Each produces a status, a progress bar, the numbers behind it and specific advice. Projections are labelled as projections and use the return you assume, never one the app promises.

Built like a vault, because it holds your money

This is the part that matters for a finance app, so the site is specific about it.

Encrypted at rest

All data sits in a SQLCipher AES-256 database. The key is 256 random bits generated on the device at first run and held in the Android Keystore or iOS Keychain, hardware-backed on most modern phones. Copying the database file off the phone yields ciphertext.

App lock

Optional PIN, hashed with PBKDF2 and a random salt, with fingerprint or face unlock on top. Wrong attempts trigger an escalating cooldown. The PIN is deliberately not the encryption key, so a short PIN cannot weaken the database.

Nothing on screen leaks

Screenshots, screen recording and the recent-apps thumbnail are blocked. A hide-amounts toggle replaces every figure with dots. Auto-lock is configurable from immediately to never.

Two network calls, both optional

Rate updates send only the currency being asked about. Drive backups send only ciphertext you hold the passphrase to. With both switched off, the app makes no requests at all. There is no analytics and no crash reporting.

Backups that only you can read

Thara can back up to a private application folder in your own Google Drive, to a folder you choose, or to a file you share anywhere. Every backup is encrypted on the phone first with a passphrase you choose. Without it the file cannot be opened, by Google, by the developer, or by anyone who finds it.

The Drive folder is hidden from the normal Drive interface and readable only by Thara. The app never asks for access to the rest of your Drive.

  1. 1
    Choose a passphraseAt least 12 characters. It is never stored in the backup and never leaves the phone.
  2. 2
    Encrypt on the deviceAES-256-GCM with a key derived by PBKDF2 at 600,000 rounds and a fresh salt every time.
  3. 3
    Upload the ciphertextInto Thara's own hidden folder on your Drive, or into a folder on the phone.
  4. 4
    Restore anywhereEnter the same passphrase on another phone. Restore runs in one transaction, so an interrupted restore leaves the old wallet intact.

Get Thara

Thara is a personal project and is not sold or listed on an app store. It is installed directly on the phone.

Android A signed APK, installed directly. Android 8 or newer. Android will ask you to allow installs from this source. Download APK
iOS An unsigned IPA. Install it with a sideloading tool such as Sideloadly using your own Apple ID. iOS 13 or newer. Download IPA

Both files are built automatically from the latest commit and published on the GitHub release page. Both platforms share the same encrypted backup format, so a backup made on one restores on the other.

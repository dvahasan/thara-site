The detail, when you want it

Open one and the last one closes, so this page never gets long again.

SecurityAES-256 at rest, the key in the phone's own keystore, and two network calls you can switch off. 4 controls
  • Encrypted at restSQLCipher, AES-256. The key is 256 bits from the device's own generator, held in the Android Keystore or iOS Keychain. Copying the file off the phone yields ciphertext.
  • App lockOptional PIN, PBKDF2-hashed with a random salt, with fingerprint or face unlock on top. Wrong attempts cool down. The PIN is deliberately not the encryption key.
  • Nothing leaksScreenshots, recording and the recent-apps thumbnail are blocked. Hide-amounts turns every figure to dots. Auto-lock from immediately to never.
  • Two network callsRate updates send only the currency asked about. The update check reads one public file a day, with no identifier. With both off, the app makes no requests at all.
Nine plansGraded against your real records, not a questionnaire you filled in once. 9 plans

Run as many at once as you like. Each gives a status, a progress bar, the numbers behind it and specific advice. Projections are labelled as projections, at the return you assume.

Backups only you can readEncrypted on the phone first, then written into a folder you chose, on a schedule. 4 steps
  1. Choose a passphraseAt least 12 characters. Never stored in the backup, never leaves the phone.
  2. Encrypt on the deviceAES-256-GCM, PBKDF2 at 600,000 rounds, a fresh salt every time.
  3. Write the ciphertextInto the folder you picked. Older automatic copies are pruned.
  4. Restore anywhereSame passphrase on another phone. Restore runs in one transaction, so an interrupted one leaves the old wallet intact.

Thara writes the file and nothing else. Whether the folder sits on the phone, an SD card or inside a cloud app you already have is your choice and that app's business. Google Drive backup is not offered at present.

Installing itAndroid is one tap. iPhone needs a computer once, and Apple is the reason. 2 platforms

Android Ready to use

  1. Open this page on the phonePress Download APK.
  2. Allow the installAndroid asks because the file did not come from the Play Store.
  3. Open itLater builds install over the top and keep your wallet.

iPhone Testing only

Apple runs only apps carrying a signature it trusts, and only a computer can ask Apple for one. Both routes use your own Apple ID; a free one lasts seven days, and renewing never touches your data.

  1. SideStoreA computer once, then the phone renews itself over Wi-Fi. sidestore.io
  2. SideloadlySimpler to set up, but a cable every seven days. Needs iTunes from Apple's own site.
  3. Trust itSettings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.
Take a backup before you reinstall anything

Renewing a signature keeps your wallet. Deleting the app does not — the encrypted database goes with it, and nobody can bring it back, the developer included.
